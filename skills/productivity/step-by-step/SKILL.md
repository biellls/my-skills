---
name: step-by-step
description: Turn a grilling session into step-by-step alignment, one step at a time from the root.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Align with the user one **step** at a time: a single idea or concept, waiting to reach alignment before moving on to the next step.

- Start from the root concept.
- Each step builds only on steps already agreed.
- Give one or more examples when the discussion will benefit from making something more concrete.
- When an agreed step reveals structure, show that slice of the agreed understanding (model, architecture, etc.) as a small ASCII diagram before the next step.
- Send fact-finding, best-practice checks and second opinions to sub-agents.
- When several possibilities open up, whether from discussion with the user or from research, present your recommendation and your reasoning for it.

Done when nothing in the design tree is left unagreed. Then summarise the whole for one final confirmation.
